“The Living Years” is a side quest for Cyberpunk 2077. V gets a seemingly normal call for a job only to realize that the person giving them that job is a child. What begins as a regular call ends up a subversion of expectations for both the player and V. 

V is tasked with finding the child’s father who had abruptly left him and his mother. What begins as a typical case of a parent abandoning their family, quickly develops into a story exploring tough choices, family love and children having to grow up to soon to be able to deal with the world around them.

The quest focuses on the weight of player choice with choices that avoid a clearly “correct” outcome.
